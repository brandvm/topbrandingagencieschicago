What we take from the references.

Clutch supplies the directory hierarchy: category, comparison and consistent company facts. Framer and Webflow show how logos and names anchor a card. Stripe shows how a compact company grid helps navigation. BYQ supplies spacing, proportions and component discipline. The five original ranking references inform long-form comparison, methodology and FAQ organisation.

Source layouts inform original code. Reference screenshots are attributed research assets on protected staging. Agency identity marks have their own source manifest. The source document remains the authority for article text, ranking order and all factual qualifications.
