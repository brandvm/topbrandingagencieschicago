Top Brand Agencies

Make the shortlist immediately legible.

Observed
A broad blue hero introduces the subject. Repeated agency blocks give names, services and outbound links a predictable hierarchy. Region and city navigation explain the scope.
Adapt
Use numbered agency names, concise fact groups and one clear next action. Keep geographic navigation limited to guides we actually have.
Apply to
Chicago: numbered profiles. Canada: geographic navigation. All six: consistent agency facts.
Keep out
Do not inherit its large menu, numerical scoring method, review claims or promotional copy.

Help readers compare before they commit.

Observed

Top Website Design Agencies

Give long-form content a useful first screen.

Observed
A dark rounded navigation bar leads to breadcrumbs, a compact topic pill, a centred sans-serif title and a bordered Quick answer panel. The reading column is restrained.

Apply to

How this research becomes our design
