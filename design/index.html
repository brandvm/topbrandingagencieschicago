08

Make the detail dependable

Content, accessibility and speed remain part of the design.

Content and interaction

  • Ten original profiles and ten FAQs per article, with supplied qualifications and source links.
  • No fabricated review counts, verification badges, dates or authors.
  • Keyboard navigation, skip links, visible focus and semantic headings.
  • Responsive company grids, contained table scrolling and reduced motion.

Delivery and performance

  • Static Astro output and locally hosted fonts, icons and company marks.
  • Production: 30KB compressed JavaScript, 40KB compressed CSS, 150KB fonts maximum per page.
  • Target 100 in Lighthouse; require median performance ≥95 and automated accessibility, best practices and SEO 100.
  • Protected noindex staging. Reference and review code stay out of production.
